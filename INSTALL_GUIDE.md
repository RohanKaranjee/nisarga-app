# 🌿 Nisarga App — Installation Guide (For Users)

> **No coding or technical knowledge required!**
> This guide will help you install the Nisarga app on your Android phone in under 2 minutes.

---

## 📱 Requirements

- ✅ An **Android phone** (Android 5.0 or higher — almost all phones after 2015)
- ✅ **Internet connection** (to download the APK file)
- ✅ About **60 MB** of free storage space

> ⚠️ **Note**: This app is currently available for **Android only**. iPhone (iOS) is not supported yet.

---

## 📥 Step 1: Download the APK

Download the Nisarga app APK file from any of these methods:

If the developer shared a **Google Drive link** or sent the file on **WhatsApp**:
1. Tap the link or open the file in WhatsApp
2. Tap **Download** ⬇️
3. Wait for the download to complete

1. Open this link in your phone's browser:
   👉 **https://github.com/RohanKaranjee/nisarga-app/releases**

3. Tap on **`app-release.apk`** to download it

### Option C: Transfer via USB / Bluetooth / Share
If someone sent you the APK file via:
- **USB cable** — Connect your phone to their computer and copy the file
- **Bluetooth** — Accept the incoming file transfer
- **Nearby Share** — Accept the share on your phone
- **Email** — Download the attachment

---

## 🔓 Step 2: Allow Installation from Unknown Sources

Since this app is not from the Google Play Store, your phone will ask for permission to install it. **This is completely safe** — it's just how Android handles apps from outside the Play Store.

### For Android 8.0 and above (most phones):

1. When you try to open the APK, your phone will show a popup:
   > **"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"**

2. Tap **"Settings"** on the popup

3. Find the toggle that says **"Allow from this source"** and **turn it ON** ✅

4. Go back and try opening the APK again

### If you can't find the setting:

1. Go to your phone's **Settings** ⚙️
2. Tap **Apps & Notifications** (or just **Apps**)
3. Tap the **three dots ⋮** at the top right → **Special Access**
4. Tap **Install unknown apps**
5. Find your **Browser** or **File Manager** in the list
6. Turn ON **"Allow from this source"** ✅

### For older Android (below 8.0):

1. Go to **Settings** ⚙️
2. Tap **Security**
3. Turn ON **"Unknown Sources"** ✅
4. Tap **OK** on the warning popup

---

## 📲 Step 3: Install the App

1. Open your phone's **File Manager** (or check your **Downloads** folder)
2. Find the file called **`app-release.apk`**
3. Tap on it
4. Tap **"Install"** at the bottom of the screen
5. Wait for the installation to complete (takes about 10-15 seconds)
6. Tap **"Open"** to launch the app! 🎉

> 💡 **Can't find the file?** 
> - Pull down your notification bar — you might see "Download complete" — tap on it
> - Or open **Google Chrome** → tap the **three dots ⋮** → **Downloads**

## ❓ Common Issues & Fixes

### ❌ "App not installed" error
**Try these fixes:**
1. Make sure you have enough **storage space** (need at least 60 MB free)
2. If you have an older version installed, **uninstall it first**, then install again
3. Restart your phone and try installing again

### ❌ "Parse error" or "There was a problem parsing the package"
**This means the APK file is corrupted or didn't download fully.**
1. Delete the downloaded file
2. Download it again with a **stable internet connection**
3. Try using a different browser (Chrome recommended)

### ❌ Chrome says "This type of file can harm your device"
**This is a standard Android warning for all APK files — it's safe!**
1. Tap **"Download anyway"** or **"Keep"**
2. Then follow the installation steps above

### ❌ "Blocked by Play Protect" or Google warning popup
1. Tap **"More details"**
2. Tap **"Install anyway"**
3. This warning appears because the app is not on the Play Store yet — it's completely safe

### ❌ App crashes on startup
1. Make sure your phone has **Android 5.0 or higher**
2. Try **restarting your phone**
3. **Uninstall** and **reinstall** the app
4. Make sure you have a working **internet connection** (the app needs internet for login)

### ❌ Can't login / Signup not working
1. Check your **internet connection**
2. Make sure your **email is valid** (e.g., example@gmail.com)
3. Make sure your **password is at least 6 characters**
4. If you forgot your password, contact the developer
